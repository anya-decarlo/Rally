# Rally — Feature Wishlist

Fun stuff we want to build. Loosely grouped, roughly in vibes order.

## Games & comparisons

- **Who wore it better** — side-by-side comparison cards: two candidates, same
  situation (a vote, a donor, a policy position). User picks. Results could feed
  the signal pipeline.
- **Who sung it better** — same idea but for soundbites/quotes: two candidates
  saying their version of the same line, user picks the better delivery.
- **Play UN** — a UN-style simulation/voting game. Users take positions on
  resolutions and see how their votes stack up against real officials' records.

## Content formats

- **Get to know the boo** — a "get to know you" card format per candidate:
  rapid-fire personal facts (the "wait, seriously?" energy but softer).
- **Menu of each problem** — an issue-by-issue menu: pick a problem
  (housing, crime, schools) and see where every candidate on the ballot stands.
- **Presidential funny clips** — pull the classic funny clips of Trump, Biden,
  and Obama off YouTube and turn them into Rally clips. Pure delight content.
- **Export stickers** — card/receipt art as exportable stickers (iMessage pack,
  shareable images).

## Social verification

- **TikTok comment rank check** — show a TikTok video's comments and let users
  compare which comments the algorithm surfaced vs. which are actually the most
  liked/replied. "Are the top comments you see the real top comments?"
